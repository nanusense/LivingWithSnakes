An on-site awareness session for residential communities – covering identification, behaviour, safety, and how to respond when a snake shows up.

60 minutes on-site session

Why this session exists

Bangalore is home to roughly 30 snake species. Most residents never encounter one – until they do, and panic fills in everything knowledge doesn't. A cobra in the garden becomes a crisis. A rat snake in the drain becomes a neighbourhood emergency.

This session gives your community what it actually needs: the ability to recognise what's there, understand why it's there, and make a clear-headed decision about what to do next.

"The snake isn't there because something went wrong. It's there because your green spaces, water features, and resident rodents make it a reasonable place to be."

What the session covers

  • Common snakes found in Bangalore apartments and layouts
  • How to tell harmless from venomous – without touching anything
  • Snake seasonality: when and why sightings spike
  • Practical safety habits for residents and domestic staff
  • What to do – and not do – when you spot one
  • How to make your home and garden less inviting to snakes
  • Live Q&A, field photographs, and real rescue case studies

The Presenter

Sandeep Nanu – Wildlife Educator & Snake Rescuer

Sandeep has documented approximately 400 snake rescues across Bangalore, with field experience spanning multiple species from rat snakes and trinkets to cobras and Russell's vipers. He runs snake awareness sessions for residential communities and schools, combining ecological precision with the kind of practical, no-nonsense framing that actually changes how people behave around wildlife.
